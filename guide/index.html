How to play Caravan Trading Game

Caravan is a free turn-based trading game you play in your browser. You lead a merchant caravan across the desert: buy goods cheap, sell them high, feed your camels and traders, and finish the journey with the biggest fortune you can.

Play Caravan for free

The goal

Be the richest trader of your faction when the journey ends. Your final score is your gold, plus the value of the goods still in your cargo, plus your crew: each trader is worth 25 points and each camel 30 points.

The rules in five steps

  1. One action per day. Each day you buy, sell, recruit or save gold, then the day ends.
  2. Buy low, sell high. Every good has a buying price, a selling price and a weight.
  3. Traders and camels. Traders earn gold every day. Camels carry more cargo.
  4. Feed your crew. Each trader and camel eats one food per day. No food, no caravan.
  5. Save gold to earn a share of your fortune. The saving rate drops each time you use it.

Game modes

Classical mode

A 15-day journey with no disasters. You start with one trader, one camel and a little food. The best way to learn the game.

Traveler mode

A 20-day journey where you build your caravan from nothing: no trader, no camel, three improvement points to spend, and a disaster on the road. Each faction unlocks its Traveler mode once you reach a high enough score in Classical mode.

Daily challenge

Every day, the same journey for every player, with one try. Compare your score on the online leaderboard.

Encounters on the road

Disasters (Traveler mode)

Near the end of a Traveler journey, fate strikes: a thief empties your pockets, a camel breaks a leg, your traders leave, the marble market collapses, a silk craze sends prices soaring… Keep some margin and adapt.

Tips for a high score

  • Recruit traders early: their daily income adds up over the whole journey.
  • Never forget food. Count how many days are left before you sell all of it.
  • Watch the weight, not only the price. Silk earns more per kilo than marble.
  • Save gold when the rate is high, because it drops every time you save.
  • Keep some gold for the Mysterious Merchant: a good item can be worth thousands of points.
  • Play each faction to its strength: spices with Mahaji, marble with Huangoz, silk with Ipartus.
