Caravan Trading Game

Lead a desert caravan from town to town. Buy goods cheap, sell them high, feed your camels and traders, and make the biggest fortune before your journey ends.

  • Turn-based trading: food, spices, pottery, marble, silk and jewelry, with prices that change every day.
  • 22 factions to unlock, each with its own rules.
  • Meet the Mysterious Merchant and the Old Witch, then face disasters in Traveler mode.
  • A daily challenge with an online leaderboard.

Free, no ads, no download. Plays on desktop and mobile.
